About Dr. Malik

Asif R. Malik, MD is a board-certified psychiatrist based in Spokane, Washington. His clinical work spans geriatric psychiatry, psychopharmacology and psychosomatic medicine. He also has extensive medicolegal experience, including Washington L&I independent medical examinations, workers’ compensation evaluations, court-ordered evaluations and expert testimony.

He holds faculty appointments at the Washington State University Elson S. Floyd College of Medicine and the Idaho College of Osteopathic Medicine.

Questions

Is this ongoing psychiatric care?

No. It is a single consultation. Your primary care clinician continues your care using the written plan.

Do you take insurance?

No. Fees are self-pay. A receipt is available in your account if you want to submit it yourself.

Can you prescribe?

When clinically appropriate and allowed by state and federal rules. Some medicines may instead be recommended to your clinician.

Why do I need to verify my identity?

To make sure the person in the visit is the patient. ID.me checks your ID and a selfie. Dr. Malik receives only confirmation that you passed.

Who can see a report requested by my employer, insurer or attorney?

Only the recipients that organization authorizes. Release is controlled case by case.

This service is not for emergencies. If you are in danger, call 911. In a mental health crisis, call or text 988.
